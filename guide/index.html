How to use the AWS Builder Center Badge Tracker

A quick walkthrough for setting up the free AWS Builder Center Badge Tracker, logging your daily activity, and following your streaks and weekly goals. Everything is saved in your own browser.

Open the tracker

1. Set up your profile

On your first visit, enter your name, AWS Builder ID, and the date you started tracking. Then tick any badges you already earned before using the tracker. Those stay completed. New streak tracking starts from today unless you add earlier activity dates yourself. You can change all of this later in Profile & settings.

2. Follow your daily routine

The Dashboard and Daily tracker use five daily activities:

  • Visit Builder Center
  • Read 1 article
  • Leave 1 meaningful comment
  • Like 2–3 pieces of content
  • Vote on a Wish

Check each one off when you finish it. Missed a day or forgot to log something? Use the Daily tracker or the Calendar to add or correct activity for any earlier date.

3. Understand daily streaks

The visit, comment, and like streaks count consecutive calendar days. The tracker shows your current and longest streak and how far you are from the 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day badges. A skipped day resets the current streak, but your longest streak is kept.

4. Track weekly goals

The 4-Week Wish Vote Streak and the 4-Week Article Publishing Streak count weeks from Monday to Sunday. One Wish vote, or one published article, in a week is enough for that week. The Weekly goals page shows what is due, a recommended window, and your last four weeks. Once you earn one of these badges, its goal disappears from the Dashboard.

To log an article, add its title and its real published date. This only counts towards the weekly streak. It does not change your First Article badge or article-like records.

5. Check your badges

The Badges page lists all 21 badges with progress, search, and filters for Quick Wins, Consistency, and 7-day or 30-day streaks. For likes-based badges such as Valued Creator, add your articles or comments with their like counts. See every badge and its requirement.

6. Back up your progress

Because data lives in your browser, clearing site data or switching browsers removes it. Open Profile & settings and use Download backup regularly. To move to a new browser or device, choose Import backup there.

Privacy: no sign-in, no AWS credentials, and no data uploads. The tracker never connects to AWS Builder Center, so you decide what to record.
