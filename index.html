What is the AWS Builder Center Badge Tracker?

A free, private tool to track your AWS Builder Center badge progress. Record daily activity, follow 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day streaks, and see how close you are to each of the 21 badges.

  • Daily tracker for visits, reading, comments, likes, and Wish votes
  • Calendar view to add or correct activity for any date
  • 4-Week Wish Vote and Article Publishing streak goals
  • Backup and restore your progress as a file

Your data never leaves your browser, and no AWS credentials are requested. Learn more in the step-by-step guide or see how it was built.
