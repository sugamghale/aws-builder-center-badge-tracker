AWS Builder Center Badge Tracker FAQ

Quick answers about the free AWS Builder Center Badge Tracker, privacy, and how streaks work.

What is the AWS Builder Center Badge Tracker?

A free, personal tool for tracking your AWS Builder Center badge progress. It records daily activity, follows streaks, tracks weekly Wish vote and article goals, and shows progress on all 21 badges.

Is it an official AWS tool?

No. It is an independent project by Sugam Ghale and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon Web Services.

Is it free, and do I need to sign in?

It is free and there is no account. You enter a name, a Builder ID label, and a start date, and the tracker is ready.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on the device you use. Nothing is uploaded, and no AWS credentials are requested.

Does it connect to AWS Builder Center and check my activity?

No. It is a manual tracker. You record what you did, so the numbers are only as accurate as your entries.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Data does not sync between browsers or devices. Download a backup in Profile & settings and import it on the other one.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your tracker data is removed with it. Download a backup regularly so you can restore your progress.

I already earned some badges. How do I add them?

Select them during first-time setup, or later in Profile & settings. Already-earned badges stay completed, and streak tracking for new activity starts from your tracking start date unless you add earlier dates.

How are daily and weekly streaks counted?

Daily streaks count consecutive calendar days. Weekly streaks count Monday to Sunday weeks, and one Wish vote or one published article in a week counts for that week.

How do I correct a missed or wrong entry?

Open the Daily tracker or the Calendar and change the activity for that date.

How many badges does it track?

21 badges: 11 Quick Wins and 10 Consistency badges. See the full list on the badges page.
