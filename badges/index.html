All 21 AWS Builder Center badges explained

The tracker follows 21 AWS Builder Center badges: 11 quick wins and 10 consistency badges. Here is what each one asks for, as listed in the tracker.

Track these badges

Quick Wins (11 badges)

Profile basics, first contributions, and the 7-day streaks.

  • Hello, World!Complete your About section.
  • Photo FinisherUpload a profile photo.
  • Discussion DebutLeave 1 comment.
  • Knowledge SeekerRead 10 articles.
  • First WishCreate your first Wish.
  • First ArticlePublish your first article.
  • Idea InfluencerGet 10 votes on your Wishes.
  • Conversation StarterGet replies on 10 of your comments.
  • 7-Day Visit StreakVisit Builder Center daily for 7 consecutive days.
  • 7-Day Comment StreakComment daily for 7 consecutive days.
  • 7-Day Like StreakLike content daily for 7 consecutive days.

Consistency (10 badges)

Weekly streaks, the 30-day and 90-day streaks, and the like-based creator and contributor badges.

  • 4-Week Wish Vote StreakVote on a Wish weekly for 4 consecutive weeks.
  • 4-Week Article Publishing StreakPublish an article weekly for 4 consecutive weeks.
  • 30-Day Visit StreakVisit Builder Center daily for 30 consecutive days.
  • 30-Day Comment StreakComment daily for 30 consecutive days.
  • 30-Day Like StreakLike content daily for 30 consecutive days.
  • 90-Day Visit StreakVisit Builder Center daily for 90 consecutive days.
  • 90-Day Comment StreakComment daily for 90 consecutive days.
  • 90-Day Like StreakLike content daily for 90 consecutive days.
  • Valued CreatorGet at least 10 likes on 5 different articles.
  • Meaningful ContributorGet at least 10 likes on 5 different comments.
Badge rewards, requirements, and eligibility can change. This list reflects what the tracker uses, so confirm the latest rules on AWS Builder Center.

How the tracker helps

Streak badges need unbroken runs, so the tracker records each day and week, shows your current and longest streaks, and highlights what is due next. Read how to use the tracker for the full routine.
